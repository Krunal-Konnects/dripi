STATEMENT OF WORK — CLINIC PORTAL V2
Synthetic sample document for the Dripi demo. Not a real contract.

Client: ClinicCo Health (fictional)
Supplier: Northwind Digital (fictional)
Signed: 2 September 2026

1. Purpose
The Supplier will design, build and deploy version 2 of the ClinicCo patient portal. The portal lets patients book appointments, view lab results and receive notifications from their clinic.

2. In-scope features
2.1 Appointment booking for up to 12 clinics.
2.2 Lab results viewer for completed results only.
2.3 Patient notifications are delivered by email only.
2.4 Reports refresh nightly.
2.5 Clinic staff sign in with email and password, with optional authenticator-app MFA.
2.6 The patient portal is delivered in English.

3. Non-functional requirements
3.1 Supported browsers: Chrome and Edge, latest two versions.
3.2 Availability target: 99.5% per calendar month, excluding planned maintenance.
3.3 Audit logs are retained for 12 months and then deleted.
3.4 Data is hosted in the client's existing cloud region.

4. Out of scope
4.1 Native mobile apps.
4.2 Integration with external pharmacy systems.
4.3 Data migration from the version 1 portal beyond active patient accounts.

5. Timeline and milestones
5.1 Design sign-off: 30 September 2026.
5.2 User acceptance testing: 2 November to 20 November 2026.
5.3 Production go-live: 30 November 2026.
5.4 Hypercare: 4 weeks after go-live.

6. Commercials
6.1 Fixed price, paid in three milestones: design sign-off (30%), UAT start (40%), go-live (30%).
6.2 Any change to scope, timeline or non-functional requirements requires a written change request approved by both parties.

7. Acceptance
The portal is accepted when all in-scope features pass UAT with no open severity-1 or severity-2 defects.
