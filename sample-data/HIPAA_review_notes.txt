COMPLIANCE REVIEW NOTES — CLINIC PORTAL V2
Synthetic sample document for the Dripi demo.
Reviewer: Compliance office, ClinicCo Health (fictional)
Date: 8 October 2026

Summary
The design meets most privacy requirements. Two items must be fixed before compliance sign-off.

Findings
1. Access logging. All access to patient records must be logged and the logs kept for at least six years. Logs must be searchable by patient and by staff member.
2. Authentication. Clinic staff accounts must use multi-factor authentication for every sign-in. Optional MFA is not acceptable for staff who can view lab results.
3. Hosting. Hosting in the client's existing cloud region is acceptable.
4. Lab results. Showing completed results only is acceptable.
5. Session timeout. Staff sessions should end after 15 minutes of inactivity.

Next steps
The Supplier should confirm how items 1, 2 and 5 will be addressed before UAT starts.
