PRD v3 — PATIENT MESSAGING AND NOTIFICATIONS
Synthetic sample document for the Dripi demo.
Owner: Product team, ClinicCo Health (fictional)
Status: Draft for review, 10 October 2026

Problem
Patients miss appointments because reminders arrive too late or go unread. Clinics want a faster way to confirm or reschedule.

Goals
- Cut no-shows by sending reminders 48 hours and 2 hours before each appointment.
- Let patients confirm, cancel or reschedule from the reminder itself.

Requirements
R1. Patients can reply to appointment reminders by SMS.
R2. Reminders are sent by email and SMS, based on the patient's saved preference.
R3. All patient-facing screens must be available in English and Spanish.
R4. The portal works on all modern browsers.
R5. Clinic managers see a dashboard of confirmations per day.
R6. Patients can download their lab results as a PDF.

Out of scope for this PRD
- Two-way chat with clinicians.
- WhatsApp messaging.

Open questions
- Which SMS provider should we use?
- Do we need patient consent text before the first SMS?
